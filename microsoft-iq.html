Scale AI using your organization’s intelligence with Microsoft IQ

a producer and a designer at Red Door; Microsoft’s marketing and product reviewers

Microsoft asked for a 14-page ebook on Microsoft IQ, due in three weeks. I was the sole writer.

Microsoft IQ is an intelligence layer across several Microsoft products. The Azure marketing team wanted to introduce it to technical decision makers. The source material included research reports, a message framework, a pitch deck, and a white paper.

I wrote a detailed outline, then the draft. Two days before the draft was due, the client asked for a component they had told me at kickoff to leave out. I raised the conflict with the client lead, who changed the earlier decision, and I added the new section. The draft was delivered on time.

After review I added a section on the cost of waiting and cut two pages in layout. Microsoft approved the ebook a day early. It became the style reference for the campaign that followed, and I wrote further pieces for that campaign.
