about

I’m a writer at Red Door Collaborative, a creative agency near Seattle.

My work includes ebooks, video scripts, infographics, articles, and strategy documents for Microsoft, Databricks, and the City of Seattle.

In 2023 I joined Microsoft on a placement writing executive communications. In 2024 I moved to the in-house team, where I help shape how the agency uses AI.

Before that I wrote for Microsoft’s Ethics & Society team, where co-authored pieces ran in ACM Interactions, the World Economic Forum, and UX Collective.
