For two years I wrote about half of a Microsoft video series, and then helped change how it gets written.

The Azure Essentials Show is a run of short interviews with Microsoft engineers and product people, published on Microsoft Learn and YouTube. I joined the writing team in mid-2024 and took about half the episodes each quarter as sole scriptwriter, from the first call with the subject expert through rehearsal and recording. The busiest stretch was five episodes written in one month ahead of a major conference.

In 2025 I standardized how episodes are built, rebuilt the script template, and trained a second writer.

In 2026 I went through the show’s whole back catalog and found that the conversational episodes did far better than the scripted ones. That backed a relaunch, with outlines in place of word-for-word scripts, written so each episode can be found and cited by AI answer engines. Most of what viewers search for isn’t about Azure, so each episode now opens on a problem they’d recognize before any product is named.

I wrote the prompt set the contract writers draft with, ran their orientation, co-recorded an onboarding video, and drafted the show’s next marketing plan.
